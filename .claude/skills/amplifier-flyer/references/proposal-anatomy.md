# Proposal anatomy

Multi page. Flows. Cream ground, black logo top right, no pink anywhere.

## Page furniture

Every page: logo at top right, header rule at y 697, footer rule at y 52,
footer line at y 40 with the document name left and `n / total` right.
Body pages also carry the partner name as a mono running header at y 706.85.

## Cover

1. Logo, header rule.
2. Meta line. `CONFIDENTIAL · FOR AUTHORIZED USE ONLY` left,
   `PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL · [MONTH YEAR]` right.
3. Partner name in Inter Bold, auto sized to the text width, one or two
   lines. Two words split one per line. This is the loudest element in the
   whole system and it is the only place type gets this large.
4. Tagline in Newsreader Italic 18. One sentence. It states what the partner
   already does that becomes a signal. "A video visit you already run becomes
   a wellness signal."
5. Rule.
6. Background box. Bordered fill, mono label, four to six lines of Inter 9.6.
   Describes the partner's business in their terms, then names the acoustic
   layer they are not reading.

## Sections

`01 02 03 04 05 07`. **There is no section 06.** Do not create one.

| Section | Title |
|---|---|
| 01 | What We've Researched On [Partner] |
| 02 | The Opportunity |
| 03 | Integration |
| 04 | Commercial |
| 05 | Why Amplifier Health |
| 07 | Get Started |

Header is mono 13.5 letter spaced, written `/ 01 TITLE` with the number at
x 54 and the title at x 96. An optional serif subhead sits 16pt under it at
x 69, in Newsreader Regular 12, either small caps style or title case.

## Blocks available

**Bullet.** Mono `/` marker at x 58, Inter 9.3 at x 69. Never a dash, never a
dot. Two lines maximum per bullet. Six bullets maximum per section.

**Stat row.** Two or three cells, 54pt tall, 3pt gaps, each with a 2pt black
cap rule. Serif value, mono label, mono sublabel in the muted tone. Use it
once per page at most. The strongest use is a zero: `0 / ACOUSTIC LAYER /
No health signal read today`.

**Callout.** Bordered fill box, mono label, Inter body. One per proposal,
near the end, carrying the single sentence that makes the partnership
specific to them.

## Writing the sections

01 is research, not pitch. Six factual bullets about the partner, each one
verifiable, no adjectives. It proves we did the work.

02 names the asset they already own and are not reading. It ends with the
Sona-2 sentence.

05 is the same five bullets every time: the model, the dataset, validation,
the evidence flywheel, the HIPAA architecture. Update the numbers, keep the
structure.
